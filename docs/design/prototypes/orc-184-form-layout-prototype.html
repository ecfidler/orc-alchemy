PROTOTYPE · ORC-184 Form Item Fields Width Theme
What to look at
  • Three layouts, switched with the bar at the bottom (or the ← → keys). A: one column with an "Add a modifier" menu that has search and groups. B: a ledger with labels on the left and a tray of modifier chips. C: a document title, sections under group headings, and a modifier checklist at the side. D: layout A, but the button opens a modal with one scrolling list of the modifiers that can be added. A click adds one.
  • Common modifiers always show (feat: race prerequisites, ability increase; race: the 7 from the modifier counts). Every other modifier is added from the list, and a remove link takes it off again (with Undo).
  • Imported opens a pack item. Its modifiers that hold a value show at once, and leave the add list.
  • An added modifier goes to its fixed place in the form, not to the end, so a built item and an imported item look the same.
  • Field styling: boxed or ruled line. Long checkbox lists use short names in a grid ("Fire", not "Resistance to fire damage"). "One number or none" groups are a segmented toggle. Feature uses are left for the uses prototype.
  • Leave artifact comments on what to keep. "The header from B with the list from A" is a good answer.
