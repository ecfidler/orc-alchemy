PROTOTYPE · ORC-191 Class Item Width Theme
What to look at
  • Three variants of the subclass Spells section, switched with the bar at the bottom or the ← → keys. Each is a table with one row for each spell level, 1st to 5th, and two spells in a row. Today at the end of the bar shows the ten labelled selects of the app today, for comparison.
  • A · Table of selects. Two selects in each row, with no label. The column headings carry the text. All ten slots are in view, also when they are empty.
  • B · Table of tokens. A spell is a token with an X, as on the other forms. The first and the second spell of a level are in two columns, so the spells line up down the table. An empty slot has a "+ Add a spell" button that opens the picker for that spell level, with search. The picker closes when the row is full.
  • C · One add button. One full-width "+ Add a spell" button under the table. Its picker lists the spells of all five levels in groups, with search, and stays open. A spell goes to the row of its level. A level with two spells leaves the list.
  • The first column is new: the class level at which the character gets the spells (paladin 3rd, 5th, 9th, 13th, 17th; cleric 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th). The form today does not show it. A warlock has no such column, because the expanded spells have no class level.
  • Class changes the section title (Spells, Domain Spells, Expanded Spells) and the first column. A spell cannot be in the two slots of one row.
