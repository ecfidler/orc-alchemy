What to try (a fighter with 44 hit points)
  • The hit points box is two buttons: the temporary part and the current part. Click the current hit points. Type 12 and select Damage: the 5 temporary hit points go first, then the hit points.
  • Temporary hit points do not add: a new amount takes the place of the old one. Set them to 8, then to 3. With "Temp HP" choice 1 the button is in the popup. With choice 2, click the Temporary part of the box. 0 or Remove takes them away.
  • Start from "Hurt", then Damage 80: instant death fills the three failure circles. A click on a circle reverses it. Heal works from Dead.
  • Start from "At 0": Damage adds one failure. Click the circles: one more, one less in each row. Three successes show Stable, and Damage while Stable clears the successes and adds a failure.
  • Turn on "Concentrating on Bless" and take damage: a note gives the save DC.
  • Set makes the current hit points equal to the number. Heal to the maximum and see the record line under the sheet: full is stored as empty.
