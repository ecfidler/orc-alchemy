What to look at
  • The prepare control has three forms. Change the form with the bar at the bottom. A: a box on the left of the row. B: a "Prepare" button on the right of the row. C: the row is the control: a click on the row prepares the spell, and on a phone the arrow on the right opens the text.
  • Each form shows a spell that is not prepared, a prepared spell, an always-prepared spell (it has a lock), a cantrip, and a known spell of a class that does not prepare.
  • The count: use "Count" above to see it under the limit, at the limit and over the limit. Over the limit, nothing stops the user and the count is an error.
  • Order while preparing: "Prepared first" puts the prepared spells at the top of each level. The order is from the moment that the user selects "Prepare spells", so a row does not move when the user clicks it.
  • Slot circles: a click on an empty circle spends one slot, and a click on a filled circle gives one back. The spent slots stay together on the left. With a mouse, the circle that will change shows before the click.
  • Pact Magic slots have three forms, under "Pact slots" above. 1: the label "Pact" and the circles in the same line as the other slots. 2 (chosen 2026-11-20): the pact slots have their own place in the Spell slots block and their own row on the sheet. They are diamonds, and the word "Pact" is in the serif font. "Wizard 9 / Warlock 3" shows this form in columns. 3: as 1, with diamonds in place of circles.
  • The circles on the sheet behind the popup work the same way. Close the popup to try them.
  • Saves: a change saves at once and the page shows nothing. Set "Saves" to "Fail" and make a change to see the failed save. "Try again" works after you set "Saves" to "Work".
  • The line under the options shows what the record holds after each change.
  • The numbers at the top of the popup for "Cleric 5 / Warlock 3" are only the cleric numbers. How the panel shows two sources is a later decision.
