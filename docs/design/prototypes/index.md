# Prototypes

Copies of the throwaway HTML prototypes that the design decisions come from. Open a file in a browser. The [Theme spec](../theme.md), the [Homebrew forms spec](../homebrew-forms.md), the [Spells panel spec](../spells-panel.md) and the [Sheet interactivity spec](../sheet-interactivity.md) say which view of each prototype is the choice. A spec wins where a prototype disagrees with it.

* [Palette](orc-144-palette-prototype.html) - The palette in light and dark (ORC-144).
* [Character sheet](orc-145-sheet-prototype.html) - The sheet layouts (ORC-145, ORC-153).
* [Stuck note and use bubble](orc-146-marks-prototype.html) - The stuck note and the use bubble (ORC-146).
* [App shell](orc-147-shell-prototype.html) - The app shell and the logo slot (ORC-147).
* [Home page](orc-150-landing-prototype.html) - The landing page with the mug stain (ORC-150).
* [Form layout](orc-184-form-layout-prototype.html) - The homebrew form layouts, field styles and the modifier list (ORC-184).
* [Uses](orc-186-uses-prototype.html) - The uses control and the sheet preview (ORC-186).
* [Pickers](orc-190-picker-prototype.html) - The pickers, tokens and background sub-selections (ORC-190).
* [Subclass spells](orc-191-spells-table-prototype.html) - The subclass spells section as a table (ORC-191).
* [Spells panel controls](orc-269-spells-panel-controls-prototype.html) - The Spells panel with the prepare control, the slot circles and the Pact Magic slots (ORC-267, ORC-269).
* [Hit points](orc-305-hit-points-prototype.html) - The hit points popups and the death save circles (ORC-305).
* [Rest dialogs](orc-309-rest-prototype.html) - The Rest button and the Short rest, Long rest and Dawn dialogs (ORC-309).
* [Cast button](orc-311-cast-prototype.html) - The Cast button and the level menu in the spell view (ORC-311).
* [Sheet layout](orc-312-sheet-layout-prototype.html) - The play controls on the sheet, with the Conditions and statuses box (ORC-312).
