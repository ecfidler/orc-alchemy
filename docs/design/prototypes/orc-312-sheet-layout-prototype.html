What to try
  • Abilities: hover an ability box, or tap it so the highlight stays. Constitution has no skill.
  • AC: click the AC box and pick a row. "AC by armor" (bottom left, or the More tab) marks the row that shows. The speed does not change.
  • Conditions and statuses: "Add condition" opens the menu. Exhaustion is in the menu. Its token has the six level circles and an X. Level 2 halves the speed (25 to 12), level 4 halves the hit point maximum, level 5 sets speed 0, level 6 shows Dead. Hover the token, or click its name, for the effects of each level.
  • Charmed says "immune". Restrained sets speed 0. A click on a token shows its rule text. "Hit points to 0" shows Unconscious with no X.
  • Uses: Divine Sense and Channel Divinity have circles. Lay on Hands is a pool with minus and plus, and a click on its number opens Spend, Regain and Set. The Hit dice box opens a small Spend popup.
  • Inspiration is a click on its box. The Rest button is beside Edit. The hit points popup, the rest dialogs and the Cast button are in the earlier prototypes.
