# Design

The settled look of the app, and the prototypes it comes from.

* [Homebrew forms spec](homebrew-forms.md) - The settled design of the homebrew content forms for M9 - layout, field styling, modifiers added from a list, pickers and tokens, feature uses with a sheet preview, suggested uses for imported content, and engine 0.4.0.
* [Theme spec](theme.md) - The settled look of Alchemy 5e for M8 - tokens, type, light and dark, the shared UI package, controls, paper patterns, notes, the app shell, Home, Preferences, and the character sheet (layout F).

# Subdirectories

* [Prototypes](prototypes/index.md) - Copies of the throwaway HTML prototypes that the M8 theme decisions and the M9 homebrew form decisions come from.
