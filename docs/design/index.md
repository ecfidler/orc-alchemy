# Design

The settled designs of the app, the draft 2024 content format, and the prototypes that the app designs come from.

* [2024 content format](2024-content-format.md) - The 2024 content format - JSON packs, two layers in each record, rules as data with named hooks, the record types and their fields, the value forms and conditions, the loader, the key scheme, the source data, attribution, and monsters - with an example species, background, feat, and spell.
* [2024 engine spec](2024-engine.md) - The spec of the 2024 rules engine in packages/engine - the engine model, the facade, the seam to the app, the content format and pipeline, the test strategy with golden characters, and the run order of the E1 build tickets.
* [Homebrew forms spec](homebrew-forms.md) - The settled design of the homebrew content forms for M9 - layout, field styling, modifiers added from a list, pickers and tokens, feature uses with a sheet preview, suggested uses for imported content, engine 0.4.0, and the Customize dialog of M5.1.
* [Spells panel spec](spells-panel.md) - The settled design of the Spells panel on the character sheet - the popup with the spell list and the spell text, prepared spells, spent spell slots in the panel and on the sheet, Pact Magic slots, how a sheet change saves, the spell records, and the run order of the build tickets.
* [Theme spec](theme.md) - The settled look of Alchemy 5e for M8 - tokens, type, light and dark, the shared UI package, controls, paper patterns, notes, the app shell, Home, Preferences, and the character sheet (layout F).

# Subdirectories

* [Prototypes](prototypes/index.md) - Copies of the throwaway HTML prototypes that the M8 theme decisions and the M9 homebrew form decisions come from.
